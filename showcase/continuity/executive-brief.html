WCS CI Sustainment & Continuity

Fal Mensah · Technical Business Integration · Digital Core / Workforce Collaboration Services · July 2026

I build the connective tissue between operational work, governance, evidence, and leadership decisions. My approach uses Jira to execute, SharePoint to retain and scale knowledge, Teams to coordinate, and AI to accelerate synthesis and drafting under human ownership.

The system, in four blocks

1 · Operational signalLive

MRT baseline established: 2,721 devices categorized across the WCS fleet. Windows evidence: 1,417 PASS · 88 FAIL, with DATA-MISSING syncs separated from true failures so the numbers are audit-grade. Room audit delivered 244 against a target of 100.

2 · Execution systemLive

Jira tracks action. SharePoint retains evidence and runbooks. Teams coordinates. AI accelerates drafting and synthesis under human review. Each platform holds one deliberate role — no duplicate process, no new standing meetings.

3 · Sustainment controlDesigned

Ownership assignments, evidence standards, continuity risks, handoff plans, and published learning — the layer that prevents a delivered improvement from fading into a one-time pilot. Designed to scale across the 21-team WCS footprint.

4 · Leadership decision surface

Risks and open decisions surfaced as data on the register, each with an owner, impact rating, and decision date — so leadership choices are explicit rather than discovered after the fact.
