## Voiceover scripts (record in Synthesia voice or your own)

**Opening (0:00)**
> Over the last three years at Lilly, I've learned that some of the most valuable
> work begins when a team can see a challenge clearly, connect the right tools,
> and turn learning into something reusable. This short showcase shares several
> initiatives I've helped bring to life, the outcomes they produced, and approaches
> that may benefit other individuals and teams. My goal isn't simply to look back —
> it's to invite conversation about what we can build next.

**Origin (0:40)**
> My door into technology wasn't the usual one. I came from social work — years of
> understanding people, barriers, and change — and entered through an apprenticeship.
> That turned out to be the differentiator, not the deficit: I translate technology
> into human language, because I had to learn it that way myself.

**DRIPS (1:05)**
> Continuous improvement was happening around us, but the evidence, ownership, and
> reuse weren't connected. So I built DRIPS: Detect, Root cause, Improve, Prove,
> Share — running on a Teams front door, Jira execution, and SharePoint proof.
> The flagship result: a meeting-room audit targeted at one hundred rooms.
> We completed two hundred forty-four, and left a runbook behind.

**Front door (2:00)**
> Before this, requests arrived from every direction and lived in people's heads.
> The front-door model gave incoming work one path: categorized, routed, owned,
> and visible. Leaders could finally see the pipeline — and delivery teams could
> finally trust it.

**Orchestration (2:35)**
> Every day brings a new AI feature, and most people either freeze or try
> everything at once. My approach: one clear job per tool. Copilot where the
> enterprise data lives. Claude for deep reasoning and building — including the
> experience you're watching right now. Copilot Studio and the agent platform for
> governed automation. The skill isn't subscribing — it's orchestration.

**Role fit (3:20)**
> Depending on where you sit — product operations, AI enablement, continuous
> improvement — different parts of this work matter to you. The showcase lets you
> pick your lens and see the evidence line up.

**Closing (3:45)**
> The common thread across my work is turning scattered needs into practical
> systems, visible evidence, and reusable learning. These examples reflect what has
> already been built — but they point toward what may be possible next. If one of
> these approaches could benefit your team, I'd welcome the chance to explore it
> together.

## B-roll prompts for Google AI Pro (Veo / Flow)

Keep all b-roll abstract — no fake offices, no fake people, nothing that could
read as a real workplace.

1. "Slow cinematic drift through a dark charcoal space with translucent glass
   panels catching warm teal and amber light, thin luminous lines connecting
   softly glowing nodes, elegant, calm, premium, no text, no people"
2. "Macro shot of warm amber light tracing a path through a dark minimal circuit
   of frosted glass tiles, teal accents, shallow depth of field, slow motion"
3. "Abstract network of five glowing nodes connecting in a loop, dark background,
   teal and warm gold palette, one node lighting at a time in sequence, seamless loop"

Use 1 under the title reveal, 3 behind section transitions if needed. Less is more.

## Assembly (Canva)

1. Import Synthesia scenes, screen captures, and Veo b-roll.
2. Timeline per the master-cut table; crossfade transitions only, 0.5s.
3. Captions on everything (auto-caption, then correct names: DRIPS, MRTS, WCS).
4. Music: one low-key ambient track, -18dB under VO.
5. Export: 1080p master, then duplicate project and trim for the 90-second cut.

## Recording the screen capture

- Open `index.html` full screen in a clean browser window (hide bookmarks bar).
- 1080p or better, cursor visible, slow deliberate clicks.
- Let the DRIPS loop animate for at least two full cycles on the Systems screen.
- On Role Fit, click two roles with a beat in between so the highlight lands.
